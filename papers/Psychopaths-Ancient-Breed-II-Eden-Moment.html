Psychopaths Ancient Breed II - The Eden moment.

I feel that some further elaboration is needed for the paper 'Psychopaths - Ancient Breed.'

In the past economic explanations of the dawn of civilization went something like this.
  1. cavemen worked in small groups.
  2. ???
  3. prehistoric man worked in large groups, grew crops, and specialized.

The Eden moment is pretty simple. But you may not feel comfortable with it without experience in economics. Don't let that dissuade you. All economics revolves around economy of scale. Economy of scale is simple. You get more work out than you put in when you specialize. But specialization requires trust of other humans to get your investment back. Why did we suddenly start trusting each other?
  • All humans were psychopaths. They lived in caves and all lied, cheated, and stole. Small groups worked together marginally.
  • A mutant (empath) baby was born. They were a psychopath with extra abilities. That offspring mated and the gene spread.
  • The empath gene forced it's humans remember how they felt with each memory and relive that feeling as they remembered events.
  • The mandatory emotional recall had several side effects.
    1. Empaths could now instantly group memories by how they felt. This greatly enhanced imagination.
    2. Empaths mandatory recall meant their logical (selfish) mind could be overridden by emotional reactions to past experience. This formed the conscience. The conscience could be used by parents to pass down a multi generational emotional map to long term risk.
    3. Empaths could read each others emotional responses when sharing memories, allow for a far greater level of trust.
  • The success of the above was heady and empowering creating a sense of irrational trust for empathic humans.
  • Irrational trust opened the door to abstract concepts like currency, contracts, and a social code.
  • The irrational trust made it easier to invest large amounts of labor in goods without fear of theft.
  • Making many of one thing means developing superior skill and tools. Gains are non linear. This is known as the economy of scale in economics.
  • Several generations after the first empath, the first civilization was born.

Why Eden?

It is the closest moment in human records to a sudden shift in human behavior leading from simple gathering behavior to civilization a short time. This biblical allegory in Genesis fits exceptionally well with retranslation of one word. Simply change the apple of 'wisdom' to the apple of 'emotional wisdom.' Regardless of faith I find this story curiously appropriate.
