‘Civilization Gene’ background

I had the misfortune of being intricately involved with a few psychopaths over my life.   I had a Eureka about them about 2 years ago.  After studying them enough to recognize them (sometimes)  I was listening to one brag about how superior they are.  I realized their boasting was a fantasy.  They claim to be superior, but they can’t do anything that non-psychopaths can’t do.  They are a behavioral subset of empaths (non-psychopaths.)  They are the last genetic step before what we think of as modern man (or woman.)

I laid this out in a paper I called ‘ancient breed.’  Ideas continue in ‘Ancient Breed II’

This lead to an the discovery that advanced psychopaths shadow Freud’s subconscious model.  And subsequently that psychopaths, without a conventional conscience are limited to two emotional primitives, despair and bliss.  The rest of us can form much more complex emotions.

Finally I examine what a society could be if corruption caused largely by psychopaths seeking golden opportunities could be eliminated.  Reaching for Utopia (and a fighting chance) is not our only benefit to this understanding.  How a person looks without a conscience, makes how our mind works more clear.

A gene or mutation creating a human conscience,  being the final prerequisite to humanity civilizing has huge implications.  I am cautious but very optimistic, as a great many things unexplained or now represented by mind numbing models simply click into place.  From economics, to psychology.  From religion and spiritual teachings to slavery and it’s societal effects.  There is little we can not discuss here.

I am am prepared for the theory behind the civilization gene to be proven wrong.  But I will be very, very surprised.   Please join me in the conversation about where we go from here.  Read the papers and lets discuss it together, right here.
